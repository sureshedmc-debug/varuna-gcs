Brand · Varuna × Nirmal Dhara

One mark for the whole project: the drone, the AI app and the boat Nirmal Dhara Jalyan, working together to clean India's rivers.

What the mark says

  • The V of VarunaTwo arms rise from one curve, giving the letter V. Varuna is the guardian of the waters.
  • A water drop, openedThe V is a drop split open at the top. It holds the river the project protects.
  • The drone keeps watchIt hovers in the opening, its camera beam on the water. It finds the plastic.
  • The AI marks itA saffron detection box closes around a floating bottle, just like the app's on-screen boxes.
  • Nirmal Dhara Jalyan collectsThe boat, with its saffron, white and green hull, heads for the bottle, its ramp lowered.
  • A clean streamThree strands of saffron, white and green flow under it: Nirmal Dhara, the pure stream.
