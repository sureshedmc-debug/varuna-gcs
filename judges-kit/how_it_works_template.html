A drone finds the plastic. Our AI marks exactly where it is. Our boat, Nirmal Dhara Jalyan, goes there and picks it up.

Step by step

What happens in one cleanup run.

  1. Plan the route

    We draw the area to search on a map in our app. The drone's autopilot flies it on its own.

    Drone

  2. Send live video

    The drone's camera streams the river to our app over a secure wireless link.

    Drone

  3. AI spots plastic

    Our trained AI model checks every video frame for bottles and trash, right on the laptop.

    AI app

  4. Pin it on the map

    Each piece of plastic gets the drone's GPS position, so we know exactly where it floats.

    AI app

  5. Boat drives there

    The app sends the pins to the boat. Its autopilot steers to each one, nearest first.

    Boat

  6. Scoop and count

    The boat's camera guides it onto the plastic, a roller ramp lifts it into the bin, and each piece is counted.

    Boat

Smart and safe

  • Stops for animalsIf a bird, dog or person is in front of it, the boat holds still until the way is clear.
  • Counts every pieceA camera above the ramp counts each item going into the bin and warns when it is nearly full.
  • A person is always in chargeOne tap stops, holds or brings either vehicle home. Both return home by themselves when the job is done.
  • Keeps a recordThe app saves what was found and collected, where and when, and turns it into a report.

What's inside

Drone

Pixhawk autopilot with GPS, a Raspberry Pi and an HD camera.

Varuna app

Runs on a laptop or phone: live map, video, our plastic-detection AI and mission control.

Boat

Nirmal Dhara Jalyan: Pixhawk autopilot with GPS, a roller conveyor ramp, a bin and a camera.
