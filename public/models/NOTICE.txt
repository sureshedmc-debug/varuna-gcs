Third-party model notice
=========================

face-yolo.onnx
--------------
YOLOv11n-face (single-class "face" detector), trained on WIDERFACE.
Source: https://github.com/akanametov/yolo-face (ONNX export mirrored at
https://huggingface.co/deepghs/yolo-face)
License: AGPL-3.0 (Ultralytics/YOLO11 family — same license family as
plastic.onnx below).

Used locally, in-browser, purely to detect human faces so the trash-detection
model's output can be filtered to exclude them. Face boxes are never shown or
reported as detections — they exist only to suppress false positives from the
trash model.

coco-yolo.onnx
--------------
YOLO11n, official Ultralytics COCO-pretrained weights (80 classes).
Source: https://huggingface.co/Ultralytics/YOLO11 (yolo11n.pt)
License: AGPL-3.0 (same family as plastic.onnx and face-yolo.onnx).

Exported locally to a fixed 320x320 ONNX graph (matching this app's other
models) and used purely as a verifier: its 'bottle' class identifies plastic
bottles specifically (trash.onnx is single-class and can't tell), and a
confident hit on any other real object suppresses an overlapping trash-model
box the same way the face model does. Runs throttled, not every frame.

plastic.onnx (+ plastic.json)
-----------------------------
Custom-trained model (YOLO11s / Ultralytics, 640x640) on the TACO litter
dataset (tacodataset.org), trained by the project owner in Colab. plastic.json lists its class
names, which classes the app reports, and the recommended confidence.
Replaces the earlier single-class trash.onnx. Not covered by the notices above.
